# Code of conduct

Settlr follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Here's the short version.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Settlr to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, background, nationality, appearance,
race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Do

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially when you disagree.
- Give constructive feedback, and take it gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the community, not only for yourself.

## Don't

- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Troll, insult, or make personal or political attacks.
- Harass anyone, publicly or privately.
- Publish someone's private information without their permission.
- Do anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@shreyashrangrej](https://github.com/shreyashrangrej),
privately through GitHub. Please don't report conduct issues in a public
issue. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Depending on the impact, the maintainer may respond with:

1. **Correction**: a private warning and an explanation of what went wrong.
2. **Warning**: a formal warning, with limited interaction for a while.
3. **Temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban**, for a pattern of violations or serious harm.

See the [full Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
for the complete text and its [FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq).
