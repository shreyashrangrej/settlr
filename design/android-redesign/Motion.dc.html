Rules
Runs on the UI thread (Reanimated worklets) at 60 or 120 fps, never on JS timers.
Interruptible: grab a sheet mid-flight and it follows your finger from where it is.
Nothing longer than 500 ms except a celebration. Lists animate on insert and remove, never on scroll.
Remove animations on, or Reduce motion in Profile: everything becomes a 150 ms cross-fade.
