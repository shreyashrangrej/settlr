The balance math is in `packages/backend/convex/lib/ledger.ts`, and the
split math for unequal group splits in `packages/shared/src/split.ts`
(`@settlr/shared/split`), which the forms use for their live preview and
Convex uses to check what it stores. All of it is integer cents.

## Friends

`otherOwes(expense)` is what the person who didn't pay owes:

- an **equal** expense: `floor(amount / 2)`, so the payer absorbs an odd
  cent;
- a **full** expense: the whole amount;
- an **exact** expense: `owedCents`, the amount typed in the dialog's "by
  amount" split (never more than the expense).

`friendDelta(entry)` is how much an entry changes what the friend owes you
(positive: they owe you more): `otherOwes` for an expense, the whole amount
for a payment, with `+` when you paid (`paidBy: 'me'`) and `−` when they
did.

The result is added to `friends.balances[currency]` in the same mutation that
writes the entry; a currency whose balance reaches zero is removed from the
record. Editing an entry applies the old entry's delta negatively and the
new one's positively.

`myFriendShare(entry)` is what an expense cost you (the amount less
`otherOwes` when you paid, `otherOwes` when they did), which the
[budget](/docs/guide/budget), All expenses and the assistant's "your share"
use.

## Groups

`shares(expense, memberOrder)` is what each member owes for an expense.
When the expense has a stored `split`, that's its `owed` map. Otherwise
it's split equally between `splitAmong`: the base share is
`floor(amount / n)`, and the leftover cents go one each to the first
participants in group-member order, so shares always sum to the total.

`applyGroupExpense(group, expense, sign)` returns the group's new totals
after adding (`sign = 1`) or removing (`sign = -1`) an expense:

- the payer's `paidCents` and each participant's `owedCents`,
- `expenseCount` and `totalCents`,
- `byCategory` and `byMonth`.

Editing an expense removes the old version and adds the new one. Callers
patch the result onto the group in the same mutation, so the totals can
never disagree with the expenses.

### Unequal splits

The expense dialog sends a `split` with the `mode` and what was typed,
never the shares themselves. `resolveSplit` in `lib/groupSplit.ts` checks it
against the group's members and works out `owed`:

| Mode | Typed per member | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `exact` | cents | Taken as typed; they must add up to the amount |
| `percent` | basis points (1% = 100) | Must add up to 100% (10,000) |
| `shares` | a weight, up to 1,000 | Any weights, like 2 for a couple and 1 for one person |
| `items` | each line's amount and who had it | Each line is split equally between the people who had it, then `extraCents` (tax and tip, negative for a discount) is spread in proportion to those subtotals. Up to 50 lines, and the lines plus the extra must equal the amount |

Percentages, shares and items go through `proportional(total, weights)`:
each member gets the floor of their exact share, and the leftover cents go
one each to the largest remainders, ties to the member who comes first in
the group, so `owed` always adds up to the amount. `splitProblem` says
what's wrong (percentages that don't reach 100, shares that don't add up
to the total); the form shows it, and Convex refuses the same cases. Only
members with a share end up in `splitAmong`.

An expense paid in another currency is converted to the group's currency
before any of this: the dialog multiplies by the rate and rounds to the
cent, and Convex checks the rate and keeps the amount and rate as paid in
`original`. The converted amount is what's split.

### Payments

`applyGroupPayment(group, payment, sign)` changes `settledCents`: up for the
member who paid, down for the one who received. A member's position is:

```ts
memberNet = paidCents - owedCents + (settledCents ?? 0)
// > 0: the others owe them; < 0: they owe
```

### Settle-up plan

`settleUp(members)` is greedy: it repeatedly matches the biggest debtor with
the biggest creditor and records a transfer of the smaller of the two
amounts. Each transfer clears at least one person, so a group of *n* members
needs at most *n − 1* payments. Settling everything with a friend
(`friends.settleEverything`) works in one currency: it records a payment
for your one-on-one balance, and the plan's transfers between the two of
you in each group you share in that currency.

```ts
// Ann paid 90 for three people; Ben and Cat owe 30 each.
settleUp([
  { id: 'ann', paidCents: 9000, owedCents: 3000 },
  { id: 'ben', paidCents: 0, owedCents: 3000 },
  { id: 'cat', paidCents: 0, owedCents: 3000 },
])
// → [{ from: 'ben', to: 'ann', amountCents: 3000 },
//    { from: 'cat', to: 'ann', amountCents: 3000 }]
```

## Monthly spending

`budgets.monthSpending` totals a month per currency from three sources:
personal expenses, `myFriendShare` of friend expenses, and your `shares`
entry of the expenses in every group you're in (as owner or linked member).
Payments are left out because they aren't spending. `expenses.month`, the
All expenses page's list, reads the same three sources and works out your
share of each expense the same way.
