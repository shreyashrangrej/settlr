The schema is in `packages/backend/convex/schema.ts`. Every row belongs to
one signed-in user, whose `userId` is their Convex `tokenIdentifier`.
Friends and group members are people the owner tracks by name; they don't
need a Settlr account, but can be linked to one.

Better Auth keeps its own tables (users, sessions, OAuth clients and
tokens) in the `betterAuth` Convex component, separate from these.

## Friends

### `friends`

A person you share one-on-one expenses with.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `userId`, `name`, `email?` | The owner, and the friend's name and optional email |
| `balances` | Currency code → cents. Positive: the friend owes you |
| `lastActivityAt` | For sorting the list |
| `linkedUserId?`, `counterpartId?` | Set once a friend request is accepted: the friend's user id and their row for you |
| `request?` | `pending` or `declined` while a request from this row is unanswered |
| `upiId?` | Their UPI id, for **Pay with UPI** when they aren't linked |
| `lastRemindedAt?` | When you last reminded them, so reminders go at most once a day |

Indexes: `by_userId`, `by_userId_and_lastActivityAt`.

### `friendEntries`

The one-on-one ledger: a union of two kinds, told apart by `kind`.

- **`expense`**: `description`, `amountCents`, `currency`, `category`,
  `paidBy` (`me` or `friend`), `split`, `date`, optional `tags`,
  `receiptId` and `recurringId`. `split` is `equal` (each owes half),
  `full` (the other person owes all of it) or `exact` (the other person
  owes `owedCents`).
- **`payment`**: `amountCents`, `currency`, `paidBy` (who handed the money
  over), optional `note`, `date`, and `recordedBy` and `unconfirmed` for a
  payment the other person recorded and the recipient hasn't confirmed yet.
  Balances already count an unconfirmed payment; rejecting it deletes it.

Both carry `userId`, `friendId` and an optional `mirrorId`. Indexes:
`by_friendId_and_date`, `by_friendId_and_amountCents`, `by_userId_and_date`,
and the `search_description` search index (filterable by `friendId`,
`category` and `paidBy`) that backs the friend page's search. Payments have
no description or category, so a search or a category filter leaves them
out.

### `friendRequests`

Sent from a friend row with an email (`fromUserId`, `fromName`,
`fromEmail`, `fromFriendId`, `toEmail`), and matched to the recipient **by
email**, so it waits for someone who hasn't signed up yet. `status` is
`pending`, `accepted` or `declined`. Indexes: `by_toEmail_and_status`,
`by_fromFriendId`.

## Groups

### `groups`

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `userId`, `name`, `currency` | The owner, and the group's one currency |
| `members` | Up to 20, each `{ id, name, paidCents, owedCents, settledCents?, userId? }`. Ids are never reused (new ones are random), since the trash and recurring templates can still name a member who left |
| `meMemberId` | The owner's member |
| `expenseCount`, `totalCents` | Running totals |
| `byCategory`, `byMonth` | Category → cents and `YYYY-MM` → cents, for insights |
| `lastExpenseAt?` | For sorting |
| `archived?` | Moved under *Archived*, out of the active list and the dashboard's totals |
| `inviteToken?`, `shareToken?` | The secrets in the join link and the read-only link; absent while a link is off |
| `budgetCents?` | A budget for the whole group, in its currency |

Indexes: `by_userId`, `by_inviteToken`, `by_shareToken`.

### `groupMembers`

One row per **linked, non-owner** member (`groupId`, `userId`), so a user
can find the groups they're in. Indexes: `by_userId`, `by_groupId`.

### `groupExpenses`

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `groupId`, `description`, `amountCents`, `category`, `date` | Amounts are in the group's currency |
| `paidBy`, `splitAmong` | A member id, and the ids of everyone with a share |
| `split?` | An unequal split (below). Without it, the expense is split equally between `splitAmong` |
| `original?` | Paid in another currency: `{ amountCents, currency, rate }`, the amount as paid and the rate used to convert it |
| `tags?`, `receiptId?`, `recurringId?` | As on the other expenses |

`split` holds `mode` (`exact`, `percent`, `shares` or `items`) and `owed`
(member id → cents, always adding up to the amount), plus what was typed so
the form can edit it again: `inputs` (cents, basis points or counts per
member), or `items` (each line's name, amount and who had it) and
`extraCents` (tax and tip, negative for a discount). Convex works `owed`
out itself from those inputs (`lib/groupSplit.ts`, see
[Balances](/docs/development/balances#unequal-splits)) and never trusts a
client's sums.

Indexes: `by_groupId_and_date`, `by_groupId_and_amountCents`, and the
`search_description` search index (filterable by `groupId`, `category` and
`paidBy`) that backs the group page's search.

### `groupPayments`

A settle-up payment between two members: `groupId`, `from`, `to` (member
ids), `amountCents`, `date`, plus `recordedBy` and `unconfirmed` (only
asked of a linked recipient who didn't record it). It changes the members'
`settledCents`, not the group's spending totals. Index:
`by_groupId_and_date`.

### `activity`

A group's log, written in the same mutation as each change: `groupId`,
`actorName`, `kind` (`expense_added`, `expense_updated`, `expense_deleted`,
`expense_restored`, `payment_added`, `payment_deleted`,
`payment_confirmed`, `member_joined`, `comment`, `group_updated`),
`description`, and optional `amountCents` and `expenseId`. Index:
`by_groupId`.

### `comments`

Comments on a group expense, oldest first: `groupId`, `expenseId`,
`userId`, `name`, `body`. Index: `by_expenseId`.

## Personal spending and budgets

### `personalExpenses`

`userId`, `description`, `amountCents`, `currency`, `category`, `date`,
optional `tags`, `receiptId` and `recurringId`. Index:
`by_userId_and_date`.

### `budgets`

One per user and currency, applying to every month: `userId`, `currency`,
`amountCents`, and optional `byCategory` (category → cents) for the
category limits. Index: `by_userId_and_currency`.

### `recurring`

A template that a daily cron (`crons.ts`, `recurring.processDue`) turns
into an expense on `nextDate`, as its owner, then moves `nextDate` on.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `userId`, `userName` | Who set it up; the expenses are added as them |
| `kind`, `friendId?`, `groupId?` | `personal`, `friend` or `group`, and where it goes |
| `description`, `amountCents`, `currency`, `category` | The expense to add |
| `paidBy?`, `split?`, `splitAmong?` | `me`/`friend` and `equal`/`full` with a friend; a member id and the members sharing it in a group |
| `frequency` | `weekly`, `monthly` or `yearly` |
| `anchorDay` | The day of the month it was set up on, so one set up on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months and goes back to the 31st after |
| `nextDate`, `paused` | The next due date, and whether it's paused |

Indexes: `by_userId`, `by_paused_and_nextDate` (what the cron reads).

### `trash`

A deleted expense, kept for 30 days: `userId`, the `description`,
`amountCents`, `currency` and `where` to list it, and `item`, a union by
`kind` (`personal`, `friend` with its `friendId`, `group` with its
`groupId`) holding the fields needed to add it back. Restoring goes
through the same code that adds an expense, so balances and totals move
back too. A daily cron (`trash.purgeExpired`) deletes rows older than 30
days, and their receipts. Index: `by_userId`.

## Files, people and messages

### `receipts`

A file in Convex storage: `userId`, `storageId`, `fileName`, `contentType`,
`size`, `attached`. It starts unattached; saving an expense attaches it, and
it's deleted with that expense: when its trash row is deleted for good,
after 30 days or from the Trash page. A scheduled function deletes receipts still
unattached a day after upload. Index: `by_storageId`.

### `profiles`

Per-user settings other people or the server need, unlike the
preferences kept in the browser or on the phone: `userId`, `name`,
`email`, and optional `upiId`, `weeklyDigest` and `inboxToken` (the
forwarding address is `<inboxToken>@INBOUND_EMAIL_DOMAIN`). Indexes:
`by_userId`, `by_inboxToken`, `by_weeklyDigest`.

### `notifications`

`userId`, `kind`, `actorName`, `read`, plus optional `description`,
`amountCents`, `currency`, `friendId`, `groupId` and `groupName`. Kinds:
`friend_expense`, `friend_payment`, `friend_entry_updated`,
`group_expense`, `group_expense_updated`, `group_payment`, `group_added`,
`request_accepted`, `reminder`, `payment_confirmed`, `payment_rejected`,
`comment` and `group_joined`. Amounts are from the recipient's side.
Indexes: `by_userId`, `by_userId_and_read`.

### `inboxItems`

A forwarded email: `userId`, `from`, `subject`, `snippet`, `status`
(`new`, `added` or `dismissed`) and the `draft` expense read from it
(`description`, and the `amountCents`, `currency`, `category` and `date`
when they were found). Index: `by_userId_and_status`.

### `usage`

Caps on what costs money or reaches other people's inboxes (`convex/usage.ts`):
`key` (`ai:<userId>`, `reminder-email:<userId>` or `signin:<email>`), the
`windowStart` of the current window and the `count` in it. One row per
key. Index: `by_key`.

### `assistantChats` and `assistantMessages`

Saved assistant conversations. A chat has `userId`, `title` and
`updatedAt` (index `by_userId_and_updatedAt`); each message has `chatId`,
`role` (`user` or `assistant`), `content`, and on replies the `memo` sent
back to the model and the `events` shown under it, as JSON (index
`by_chatId`).

## Linked friends

When a friend request is accepted, the two `friends` rows are linked
(`linkedUserId`, `counterpartId`) and each side's history is copied into the
other. From then on every entry is **mirrored**: it's written once in each
ledger, from that person's side (`paidBy: 'me'` in one is
`paidBy: 'friend'` in the other), and `mirrorId` pairs the twins. Each user
still only reads their own rows. The twins share one receipt.
`lib/friendship.ts` writes and updates the twins.

Deleting a friend deletes your rows; the other person keeps theirs as an
unlinked friend.

## Linked group members

A group belongs to its owner. Linking a member to a friend's account, or
someone joining through the invite link, sets `members[].userId` and adds a
`groupMembers` row, which is how that user's queries find the group.
Linked members can read the group and add expenses and payments; only the
owner can change or delete the group.

## Conventions

- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, months `YYYY-MM`.
- Money is integer cents (`amountCents`), checked by `input.amount`: a
  positive integer up to 100,000,000.
- Tags (`input.tags`): up to 5 per expense, each up to 24 characters,
  stored lowercase with spaces as hyphens and without duplicates.
- Lists that grow with "Show more" take a `limit`, clamped to 1–500.
- Queries use indexes (`withIndex`) rather than filters, and cap how much
  they read (`take`).
