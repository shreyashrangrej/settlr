Settlr has an Android app. It uses the same account and the same data as
the website, so anything you add or change on one shows up on the other
straight away.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Download the APK

    On your phone, open the [latest release](https://github.com/shreyashrangrej/settlr/releases/latest)
    on GitHub and download `settlr-v….apk` under **Assets**. The app isn't on
    the Play Store yet.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Install it

    Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow
    installs from your browser or Files app: allow it, then choose
    **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Choose **Continue with Google**, or continue with your email and type in
    the 6-digit code Settlr emails you. Sign in with the same account you
    use on the website. The app keeps you signed in until you sign out from
    **Profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To update, download the newer APK from the releases page and install it
over the old one.

## Find your way around

The bar along the bottom has **Home**, **Groups**, a **+** in the middle
that adds an expense, **Friends** and **Spending**. On Home, the bell opens
your [notifications](/docs/guide/notifications) and your avatar opens
**Profile**.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your overall balance, what you're owed and what you owe, who to **Settle next**, your balances, this month's share against your budget, upcoming bills and recent expenses. The **Ask** bar opens the [AI Assistant](/docs/guide/assistant) |
| **Groups** | Your groups, with archived ones at the end. A group shows its expenses by day, your balance and its budget; **Balances** has everyone's balance, the settle-up plan, reminders and payments waiting for you to confirm |
| **Friends** | Friend requests, then the friends you have a balance with and the ones you're settled with. A friend's page has your expenses and payments by month, **Settle up**, **Remind** and **Across everything** |
| **Spending** | The month, for **Everything** or just **Personal**: your share against the budget, where it went, by category and by tag, and the month's expenses. Tap the budget card to set your monthly [budget](/docs/guide/budget) and category limits |

## Add an expense

Tap **+**. Type the amount on the keypad first, then fill in:

- **where it goes**: just you, a friend or a group,
- **what it was**: Settlr guesses the category from the description, and
  you can change it,
- the **date**, **who paid** and **how it's split**: in a group equally,
  by amounts, by % or by shares; with a friend equally, owed in full, or an
  amount.

Tap an expense anywhere in the app to see who paid, how it's split, its
receipt and (in a group) its comments, and to **Edit** or **Delete** it.
Deleting moves it to the [Trash](/docs/guide/trash) for 30 days, and the
message that confirms it has **Undo**.

## Settle up

**Settle up** on a friend, or **Record** on a row of a group's settle-up
plan, opens a sheet: who pays whom, the full balance or part of it, the
date and a note. When you owe someone in INR who has a UPI id, **Pay with
UPI** opens your UPI app with the amount filled in. Then slide **Slide to
record** to save the payment. See [Settling up](/docs/guide/settling-up).

## Profile

- **Preferences**, kept on this phone: your default currency, appearance
  (light, dark, or following the phone), **Reduce motion** and **Haptics**.
- **Account**, the same on every device: your UPI id, the weekly digest
  email and your budget.
- **AI apps**: the connector URL to [use Settlr from Claude or
  ChatGPT](/docs/guide/mcp).
- **Help & docs** opens this site, and **Sign out**.

## On the website only

These aren't in the Android app yet, so use the website for them:
attaching or scanning receipts, splitting by items, paying in another
currency, [recurring expenses](/docs/guide/recurring), the [monthly
recap](/docs/guide/recap), the [email inbox](/docs/guide/inbox), the
[Trash](/docs/guide/trash) page, invite and share links, group insights and
activity, [importing from Splitwise and exporting
CSV](/docs/guide/import-export), and saved assistant chats.
