The **Assistant** page is a chat for your expenses. Write what you'd tell a
friend, and it makes the changes through the same checks as the forms.

## What it can do

<Tabs items={['Add', 'Find', 'Change', 'Create']}>
  <Tab value="Add">
    - "Add tea personal expense 50"
    - "Lunch with Sam 24, I paid, split equally"
    - "Taxi 600 in the Goa trip, paid by Priya"
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Find">
    - "How much did I spend on food this month?"
    - "Show my expenses with Sam"

    Results list the matching expenses with totals and your share.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Change">
    - "Change the tea to 60"
    - "Make yesterday's taxi a transport expense"
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Create">
    - "New group Pune stay with Vaishnavi, hotel 2000 split equally"
    - "Add Alex as a friend"

    It can create a group and add expenses to it in one reply.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Under each reply, Settlr shows what actually happened: the expenses added,
changed or found, and links to new groups and friends.

## Deleting

The assistant never deletes anything on its own. When you ask it to, it
shows the expense with a **Delete** button, and nothing is deleted until you
press it.

## Good to know

- It knows your friends' and groups' names, so say "with Sam" or "in the Goa
  trip".
- It doesn't guess amounts or who an expense is with: if something is
  missing, it asks.
- Dates default to today, and the currency to your default currency.
- The conversation lives in your browser and isn't saved.
- Settle-up payments, receipts, and moving an expense between personal, a
  friend and a group are done in the app itself.

<Callout type="info">
  The assistant runs on a language model through OpenRouter. If the page says
  it isn't set up, the deployment is missing `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` and
  `OPENROUTER_MODEL` (see [Environment variables](/docs/development/environment)).
</Callout>
