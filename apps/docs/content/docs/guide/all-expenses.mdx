The **All expenses** page lists every expense you're part of, wherever it
lives: in your groups, with friends and your personal spending. Settle-up
payments aren't expenses, so they're left out.

## Browse by month

The page shows one month at a time; use the arrows to move between months.
At the top you see what the month cost you (**your share**, in each
currency you spent in) and how much of it was in groups, with friends and
personal. Beside the list, **By category** breaks your share down.

## The list

Each expense shows its date, **where** it lives (the group, the friend, or
Personal), who paid, and the amount. When you didn't cover all of it, the
line under the amount shows **your share**.

Search the month's expenses, filter them by **category** or by where they
live (**In groups**, **With friends**, **Personal**), and sort them by
**date** or **amount**. The figures at the top still cover the whole month.
The list grows with **Show more**.

To change or delete an expense, open it where it lives: its name, or
**Open in …** in its **⋯** menu, goes to the group, friend or Personal
page it belongs to.
