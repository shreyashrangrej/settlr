**Monthly recap** shows what you spent in a month (your share, across
groups, friends and personal), how that compares with the month before,
your top categories and the biggest expenses. Step through months with the
arrows.

To get a summary by email, turn on the **weekly digest** under **Settings →
Account**: every Monday it sends what you spent last week and where your
balances stand.
