The **Friends** page is for expenses with one other person, outside any
group: the lunch you paid for, the taxi you shared.

## Add a friend

Choose **Add friend** and enter their name. A friend is just someone you track
by name: they don't need a Settlr account.

If you also enter their **email**, Settlr sends them a friend request (see
[Shared ledgers](#shared-ledgers) below). You can add the email later and
send the request from the friend's page.

You can have up to 500 friends, each with a different name and email.

## Add an expense

Open a friend and add an expense with a description, amount, currency,
category and date. Then say:

- **Who paid**: you or your friend.
- **How to split it**:
  - **Split equally**: each of you owes half.
  - **Owed in full**: the person who didn't pay owes all of it. "I paid for
    Sam's lunch" is *you paid, owed in full*.

On an equal split of an odd amount, the person who paid absorbs the extra
cent: 10.01 split equally means the other person owes 5.00.

Every expense can be edited or deleted later, and can carry a
[receipt](/docs/guide/receipts).

## Balances

Each friend shows what they owe you or what you owe them, **per currency**:
if you share expenses in USD and EUR, you see two balances. Amounts owed to
you are blue and amounts you owe are orange, always with a word or sign
beside them.

## Settle up

When one of you pays the other back, choose **Settle up** and record the
payment: who handed the money over, the amount, the currency and an optional
note. Payments reduce the balance; they don't count as spending.

## Shared ledgers

When a friend accepts your friend request, your two ledgers are linked:

- You both see the same expenses and payments, each from your own side. If
  you paid and split equally, your friend sees that they owe you half.
- Anything either of you adds, edits or deletes shows up for the other, and
  they get a [notification](/docs/guide/notifications).
- History from before the link is copied both ways, so nothing is lost.

Requests are matched by email, so a request waits for someone who hasn't
signed up yet. If they decline, you can send it again from the friend's page.

## Remove a friend

Deleting a friend deletes your ledger with them. If you were linked, they
keep their copy of the history, as a friend who is no longer linked.
