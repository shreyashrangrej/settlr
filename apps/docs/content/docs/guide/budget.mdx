The **Budget** page counts everything you spend in a month:

- your **personal** expenses,
- your **share** of expenses with friends,
- your **share** of expenses in every group you're in, as owner or linked
  member.

Settle-up payments aren't spending, so they don't count.

![The Budget page: what you spent by source, and progress against a $2,500 budget](/screenshots/budget.png)

## Set a budget

Set an optional monthly amount per currency. A budget applies to every
month, and you can have one for each currency. Remove it at any time.

## What you see

For the month, per currency:

- **progress** against your budget,
- **what's left**, and a **daily allowance** for the rest of the month,
- **where the money went**.

Use the month navigation to look back at earlier months.
