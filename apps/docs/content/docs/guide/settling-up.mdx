## Pay with UPI

In India, add your UPI id under **Settings → Account**. When someone owes you
in INR, their settle-up row shows **Pay with UPI**: on a phone it opens
GPay, PhonePe or any UPI app with the amount filled in, and on a computer it
shows a QR code to scan. For a friend who isn't on Settlr, save their UPI id
on their page. UPI apps don't report back, so record the payment in Settlr
afterwards.

## Reminders

When a friend owes you, **Remind** on their page sends them a notification
if they're on Settlr, or an email if you have their address (once a day at
most). In a group, the bell beside a settle-up row reminds a member who owes
you.

## Confirming payments

When someone records that they paid you, you're asked **Did you get paid?**.
**Got it** confirms it; **Didn't get it** removes it and puts the balance
back. The balance moves as soon as it's recorded, and whoever recorded it is
told what you answered.

## Settle everything with a friend

For a friend who's on Settlr, **Across everything** adds your one-on-one
balance to what the settle-up plans say between you in every group you
share. **Settle everything** records a payment in each place, so it's all
square in one go.
