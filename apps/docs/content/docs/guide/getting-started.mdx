## Sign in

Settlr has no passwords, and the same form signs you in or creates your
account. On the home page, either:

- choose **Continue with Google**, or
- enter your **email address**, choose **Email me a code**, then type in
  the 6-digit code Settlr emails you and choose **Verify and sign in**.
  Codes expire after 5 minutes; **Resend code** sends a new one, and
  **Different email** goes back.

Signing in with Google and with an email code for the same address lands on
the same account. If your account has no name yet (as when you first sign in
with an email code), Settlr asks for one: it's what friends and group
members see.

## Find your way around

After signing in you land on the **dashboard**. Across the top are your
net balance, what you're owed, what you owe and your share of this month's
spending. Below them are your balances with friends and groups, the month's
latest expenses and your groups, and beside those your share by category,
your budget, **To settle** (what you owe, with a button to settle each),
**Upcoming bills** from your [recurring expenses](/docs/guide/recurring)
and a **Quick split** form for your groups.

![The dashboard: net balance and this month's share, balances with friends and groups, the month's share by source, the budget and what's left to settle](/screenshots/dashboard.png)

The sidebar has the other sections, and the top bar shows where you are. On
phones the main sections are in the tab bar along the bottom, and **More**
(or the menu button at the top left) opens the full sidebar.

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [All expenses](/docs/guide/all-expenses) | Every expense you're part of, month by month |
| [Friends](/docs/guide/friends) | One-on-one expenses and payments with a single person |
| [Groups](/docs/guide/groups) | Shared expenses with up to 20 people, and a settle-up plan |
| [Personal](/docs/guide/personal) | Your own spending, month by month |
| [Budget](/docs/guide/budget) | A monthly budget across everything you spend |
| [Recurring](/docs/guide/recurring) | Rent, subscriptions and other bills Settlr adds for you |
| [Monthly recap](/docs/guide/recap) | A month in review next to the month before |
| [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox) | Receipts and bills you forwarded by email, as draft expenses |
| [Trash](/docs/guide/trash) | Deleted expenses, restorable for 30 days |
| [AI Assistant](/docs/guide/assistant) | Add, find and change expenses by chatting |
| [Notifications](/docs/guide/notifications) | Friend requests and activity from other people (the bell in the top bar) |

**Settings**, at the foot of the sidebar and in the account menu, has your appearance,
default currency, account settings (UPI id, weekly digest, inbox address)
and the AI apps you've connected.

To edit or delete an expense, open the **⋯** menu at the end of its row.

The **New** button in the top bar (and **Add expense** on the dashboard)
opens a menu: *In a group*, *With a friend*,
*Just for me* or *Ask the assistant*.

<Callout title="Keyboard shortcut">
  On a wide screen, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>
  on a Mac) to collapse the sidebar to a rail of icons. Settlr remembers your choice.
</Callout>

## Currencies

Settlr supports **USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD and AUD**. Every amount is kept in
whole cents, so splits always add up exactly.

- Personal expenses and expenses with friends can each be in any currency.
  Balances with a friend are kept separately per currency.
- A group has one currency, set when you create it. It can only change while
  the group has no expenses.

Pick the currency new expenses start with under **Settings → Defaults →
Default currency**. That preference is saved in your browser, so set it on
each device you use.

## Categories

Every expense has one of seven categories, which drive the totals on the
personal, budget and group insights pages: food, groceries, transport,
lodging, utilities, entertainment and other.

## Appearance

Settlr follows your device's light or dark setting. To pick one yourself,
use the sun/moon button in the top bar, **Dark mode** or **Light mode** in
the account menu, or **Settings →
Appearance → Theme**, where **System** goes back to following your device.
The new theme spreads out in a circle from whichever control you pressed.

**Settings → Appearance** also has:

- **Accent colour**: indigo (the default), blue, violet, teal, rose or
  graphite, for buttons, links, focus rings and selection.
- **Frame**: on wide screens, the sidebar, top bar and the rim around the
  page are **Tinted** with your accent colour, or **Match page** to give
  them the page's own colour.
- **Motion**: **Reduce** turns off the animations (pages rising in, lists,
  dialogs). **Follow system** does that when your device asks for reduced
  motion.

These apply right away and, like the default currency, are saved in your
browser.
