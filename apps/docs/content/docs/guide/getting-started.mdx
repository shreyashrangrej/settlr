## Sign in

Settlr has no passwords. On the home page, either:

- **Continue with Google**, or
- enter your **email address** and type in the 6-digit code Settlr emails
  you. Codes expire after 5 minutes.

Signing in with Google and with an email code for the same address lands on
the same account. If your account has no name yet (as when you first sign in
with an email code), Settlr asks for one: it's what friends and group
members see.

## Find your way around

After signing in you land on the **dashboard**, an overview of your balances
with friends, your groups and this month's spending.

![The dashboard: net balance, balances with friends and groups, quick split and what you owe](/screenshots/dashboard.png)

The sidebar (a tab bar on phones) has the other sections:

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Friends](/docs/guide/friends) | One-on-one expenses and payments with a single person |
| [Groups](/docs/guide/groups) | Shared expenses with up to 20 people, and a settle-up plan |
| [Personal](/docs/guide/personal) | Your own spending, month by month |
| [Budget](/docs/guide/budget) | A monthly budget across everything you spend |
| [Assistant](/docs/guide/assistant) | Add, find and change expenses by chatting |
| [Notifications](/docs/guide/notifications) | Friend requests and activity from other people |

The **Add expense** button opens a menu: *In a group*, *With a friend*,
*Just for me* or *Ask the assistant*.

<Callout title="Keyboard shortcut">
  On a wide screen, press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd>
  on a Mac) to collapse the sidebar to a rail of icons. Settlr remembers your choice.
</Callout>

## Currencies

Settlr supports **USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD and AUD**. Every amount is kept in
whole cents, so splits always add up exactly.

- Personal expenses and expenses with friends can each be in any currency.
  Balances with a friend are kept separately per currency.
- A group has one currency, set when you create it. It can only change while
  the group has no expenses.

Pick the currency new expenses start with under **Settings → Defaults →
Default currency**. That preference is saved in your browser, so set it on
each device you use.

## Categories

Every expense has one of seven categories, which drive the totals on the
personal, budget and group insights pages: food, groceries, transport,
lodging, utilities, entertainment and other.

## Appearance

Settlr opens in its dark theme. Switch to light (or back) from the account
menu.
