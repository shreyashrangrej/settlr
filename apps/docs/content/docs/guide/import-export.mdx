## Import from Splitwise

In Splitwise, open the group and export it as a spreadsheet. In Settlr,
choose **Groups → Import from Splitwise**, pick the file, say which person in
it is you, and import. Expenses keep their exact shares, payments come over
as payments, and everything is tagged `splitwise`. Rows paid by more than
one person can't be represented and are listed before you import.

## Export to CSV

**All expenses → Export** downloads the month on screen; a group's **⋯ →
Export CSV** downloads the whole group. Each row has the date, description,
category, where it was, who paid, the amount, your share and its tags.
