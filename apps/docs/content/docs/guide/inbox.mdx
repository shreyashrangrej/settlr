---
title: Email inbox
description: Forward receipts and bills to Settlr and add them as expenses.
icon: Inbox
---

Under **Settings → Account**, create your forwarding address. Forward
receipts, bills and order confirmations to it: each email shows up on the
**Inbox** page as a draft expense with the amount, date and category Settlr
read from it. Check it, then **Add as personal expense** or **Dismiss**.

Keep the address private. **Make a new address** stops the old one working.
