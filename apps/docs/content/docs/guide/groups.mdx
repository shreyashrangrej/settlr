Groups are for trips, flats, dinners and anything else you share with
several people.

## Create a group

Choose **New group** and give it a name, a currency and its members. You're
always a member, and a group can have up to 20 people. Members are names:
they don't need a Settlr account.

## Add an expense

Choose **Add expense** at the top of the group page. It opens the same
expense dialog as everywhere else in Settlr (editing an expense from its
**⋯** menu opens it too). A group expense has a description, amount,
category, date and optional receipt like any other, plus:

- **Paid by**: the member who paid.
- **Split**: who shares it, and how:
  - **Equally** between the members you tick, to the cent. When the amount
    doesn't divide evenly, the leftover cents go to the first members in
    the group's order, so the shares always add up to the total.
  - **Amounts**: type what each person owes; they must add up to the total.
  - **%**: percentages that add up to 100.
  - **Shares**: weights, like 2 for a couple and 1 for one person.
  - **Items**: list the bill's lines and tick who had each; tax, tip or a
    discount is spread in proportion to what each person had.

Group expenses are always in the group's currency. As you fill it in, the
dialog shows each member's share and your balance in the group before and
after the expense. If you paid in another currency, tick **Paid in another
currency**, enter what you paid and the rate (**Get rate** fetches the
European Central Bank's rate for that day); the converted amount is what's
split.

Expenses can also carry **tags**, be filled in from a receipt photo
(**Fill from receipt**), and **repeat** (the **Doesn't repeat** chip; see
[Recurring expenses](/docs/guide/recurring)).

## Find expenses

The group page lists its expenses with filters for **category** and **who
paid**, a search on the description, and sorting by **date** or **amount**.
Filters live in the page's URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
The list grows with **Show more**.

## Balances and the settle-up plan

For every member, Settlr keeps what they paid, their share of the expenses
and the payments they've made or received. A member's balance is:

```text
paid − share of expenses + payments made − payments received
```

Positive means the others owe them; negative means they owe.

![A group's page: its expenses, each member's balance and the settle-up plan](/screenshots/group.png)

The **settle-up plan** lists payments that clear every balance. It
repeatedly matches the person who owes the most with the person owed the
most, so a group of *n* people never needs more than *n − 1* payments.

## Record a payment

Any member can record a payment: from a row of the settle-up plan, or with
**Settle up** at the top of the group page for any amount between two
members. Payments change balances but not the group's spending
totals. A payment can be deleted if it was a mistake.

## Insights

The figures across the top of a group (total spent, your balance, the
number of expenses and members) show on its expenses and on **Insights**,
which you open with the **Insights** button at the top. Insights breaks
the group's spending down **by category** and
**by month**, and compares what each member **paid** with **their share**.

![Group insights: totals, spending by category and by month](/screenshots/group-insights.png)

## Edit the group

The group's owner can rename it and add, rename or remove members. A member
can only be removed if they aren't on any expense or payment, and the
currency can only change while the group has no expenses.

## Share a group with friends

The owner can link a member to a [friend](/docs/guide/friends) who has
accepted a friend request. A linked member:

- sees the group in their own Groups list,
- can add expenses and record payments,
- is notified when they're added, and when expenses or payments involve them.

Only the owner can edit or delete the group itself.

## Invite people

In the group's **⋯** menu, **Invite and share links** lets the owner turn
on a **join link**. Anyone
who opens it signs in and joins, either as a member already in the list
(keeping that member's expenses and balance) or as a new member. Turning the
link off, or making a new one, stops the old link working.

## Share a read-only view

The same dialog has a **read-only link**: a page with the balances, the
settle-up plan and the latest expenses that anyone can open without signing
in. Nobody can change anything there.

## Activity and comments

**Activity**, a button at the top of the group, lists everything that
happened in it: expenses added,
edited, deleted and restored, payments, comments and people joining. Each
expense has a comment thread (row menu → **Comments**); people who are part
of the expense are told about new comments.

## Budget, export and archive

- **Set a budget** (owner): a total for the group, like a trip budget. The
  group page shows how much is left.
- **Export CSV**: every expense and payment in the group as a spreadsheet.
- **Archive** (owner): once everyone is settled up, move the group under
  *Archived* on the groups page. Unarchive it at any time.

Deleted expenses go to the [Trash](/docs/guide/trash) for 30 days.
