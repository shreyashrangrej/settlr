Groups are for trips, flats, dinners and anything else you share with
several people.

## Create a group

Choose **New group** and give it a name, a currency and its members. You're
always a member, and a group can have up to 20 people. Members are names:
they don't need a Settlr account.

## Add an expense

A group expense has a description, amount, category, date and optional
receipt like any other, plus:

- **Paid by**: the member who paid.
- **Split among**: the members who share it. It's split **equally** between
  them, to the cent. When the amount doesn't divide evenly, the leftover
  cents go to the first members in the group's order, so the shares always
  add up to the total.

Group expenses are always in the group's currency.

![Adding a group expense: amount, category, who paid, and who it's split between](/screenshots/group-expense.png)

## Find expenses

The group page lists its expenses with filters for **category** and **who
paid**, a search on the description, and sorting by **date** or **amount**.
Filters live in the page's URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.
The list grows with **Show more**.

## Balances and the settle-up plan

For every member, Settlr keeps what they paid, their share of the expenses
and the payments they've made or received. A member's balance is:

```text
paid − share of expenses + payments made − payments received
```

Positive means the others owe them; negative means they owe.

![A group's page: its expenses, each member's balance and the settle-up plan](/screenshots/group.png)

The **settle-up plan** lists payments that clear every balance. It
repeatedly matches the person who owes the most with the person owed the
most, so a group of *n* people never needs more than *n − 1* payments.

## Record a payment

Any member can record a payment, straight from the plan or for any amount
between two members. Payments change balances but not the group's spending
totals. A payment can be deleted if it was a mistake.

## Insights

The **Insights** tab breaks the group's spending down **by category** and
**by month**, and compares what each member **paid** with **their share**.

![Group insights: totals, spending by category and by month](/screenshots/group-insights.png)

## Edit the group

The group's owner can rename it and add, rename or remove members. A member
can only be removed if they aren't on any expense or payment, and the
currency can only change while the group has no expenses.

## Share a group with friends

The owner can link a member to a [friend](/docs/guide/friends) who has
accepted a friend request. A linked member:

- sees the group in their own Groups list,
- can add expenses and record payments,
- is notified when they're added, and when expenses or payments involve them.

Only the owner can edit or delete the group itself.
