---
title: Trash and undo
description: Deleted expenses can be restored for 30 days.
icon: Trash2
---

Deleting an expense moves it to the **Trash**, and the toast that confirms
it has **Undo**. From the Trash page you can **Restore** it, balances and
all, or delete it for good. After 30 days it's deleted for good with its
receipt.

An expense can't come back if its friend or group was deleted, or if someone
on it has since left the group. Settle-up payments are deleted outright.
