---
title: Recurring expenses
description: Rent, subscriptions and other bills that Settlr adds for you.
icon: Repeat
---

When you add an expense (personal, with a friend or in a group), pick how
often it **repeats**: every week, month or year, starting from the expense's
date. Settlr adds it on each due date, as you; a monthly one set up on the
31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

The **Recurring** page lists them with their next date. Pause one to skip
dates (resuming carries on from the next date), or stop it; expenses it
already added stay. The dashboard's **Upcoming bills** shows what's due in
the next 30 days.

Repeating expenses are split equally (or owed in full with a friend), in the
group's currency.
