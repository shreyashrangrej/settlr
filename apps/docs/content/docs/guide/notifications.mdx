The bell in the top bar shows how many unread notifications you have. It
opens a dropdown with the latest ones; **More** opens the full list on the
**Notifications** page.

![The Notifications page: a friend request to accept, and activity from a linked friend](/screenshots/notifications.png)

## What you're notified about

- A **friend request**, which you can accept or decline right there.
- Someone **accepted** your friend request.
- A linked friend **added or edited an expense** with you, or **recorded a
  payment**.
- You were **added to a group**.
- Someone **added or edited a group expense** that involves you.
- Someone **recorded a group payment** with you ("Priya paid you").

Amounts are from your side: your share of an expense, or the payment amount.

## Accepting a friend request

Accepting links your ledgers (see
[Shared ledgers](/docs/guide/friends#shared-ledgers)). If you already track
that person under the same email, Settlr reuses that friend, so your
existing history is kept and shared. If you had also sent them a request,
it's answered at the same time.
