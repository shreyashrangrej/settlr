The **Personal** page is for spending that involves nobody else: groceries,
a coffee, your phone bill.

## Add an expense

Add a description, amount, currency, category and date, and optionally a
[receipt](/docs/guide/receipts). Personal expenses can be in any supported
currency.

## Browse by month

The page shows one month at a time; use the arrows to move between months.
For each month you see:

- the month's **total** in each currency you spent in,
- totals **by category**,
- the expenses, newest first, each of which can be edited or deleted.

![The Personal page for November: totals, the expense list and spending by category](/screenshots/personal.png)

<Callout>
  Personal totals only count personal expenses. To see everything you spent,
  including your share of friend and group expenses, use the
  [Budget](/docs/guide/budget) page.
</Callout>
