Settlr keeps track of who paid for what and who owes whom. You add an expense
once, Settlr splits it to the cent, and when it's time to pay back it works
out the fewest payments that clear every balance.

It covers three kinds of spending:

- **With a friend**: one-on-one expenses with a single person, outside any group.
- **In a group**: trips, flats and dinners with up to 20 people.
- **Just for you**: your own spending, month by month, with an optional budget.

You can add expenses through forms, by chatting with the built-in
[assistant](/docs/guide/assistant), or from Claude, ChatGPT and other AI apps
through Settlr's [MCP server](/docs/guide/mcp).

## For developers

Settlr is an open-source [Turborepo](https://turborepo.com) monorepo: a
Next.js 16 web app, a [Convex](https://convex.dev) backend with
[Better Auth](https://better-auth.com) for sign-in, and these docs, built
with [Fumadocs](https://fumadocs.dev).
